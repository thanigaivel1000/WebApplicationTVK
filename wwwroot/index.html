CM Oath Ceremony

📍 Venue: Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium, Chennai
🕘 Time: 10:00 AM
🎤 Vijay officially took oath as the 13th Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu after securing alliance support from Congress, CPI, CPI(M), VCK and IUML.
🏛️ TVK emerged as the single largest party with 108 seats and crossed the majority mark with support from 120 MLAs.
🤝 PM Narendra Modi, Rahul Gandhi, MK Stalin and several national leaders congratulated Vijay after the swearing-in ceremony.
⚡ Vijay signed his first major order announcing 200 units of free electricity for eligible households.
🚨 717 TASMAC shops near schools, temples and bus stands were ordered to shut down as part of the new reform initiative.
👥 Massive celebrations erupted across Tamil Nadu with TVK workers distributing sweets and bursting crackers.
✨ Vijay became the first Tamil Nadu Chief Minister since 1967 not belonging to either DMK or AIADMK.
